## Prior work — do not present as new / 已知结果，请勿称为本文新定理

The Hilbert series used in the last step of the proof is equivalent to known results. The multilinear components of the enveloping algebra of the ideal generated by the E_i coincide with the homology of the Losev–Manin spaces: their Betti numbers are due to Losev and Manin, *Michigan Math. J.* 48 (2000), and the algebra structure was presented by Dotsenko, Shadrin and Tamaroff, *Comm. Math. Phys.* 399 (2023). The paper explains this relation and also gives an independent, self-contained derivation using a PBW basis of U(g) obtained from Bergman’s diamond lemma and a sign-reversing involution.

Those dimension counts, Hilbert series, PBW / Eulerian identifications, and the Losev–Manin / DST dictionary **must not be described as new results of this paper**. The author states that an affirmative answer to Tao’s question, and the freeness statement, were not found in the literature searched; no stronger novelty claim is made.

证明最后一步所用的 Hilbert 级数等价于已知结果；由 E_i 生成的理想的包络代数的多重线性分量与 Losev–Manin 空间的同调一致（Betti 数属于 Losev–Manin，代数结构由 Dotsenko–Shadrin–Tamaroff 给出）。本文说明了这一联系，并用 Bergman 钻石引理得到的 U(g) 的 PBW 基给出了独立推导。上述维数、Hilbert 级数、PBW / Eulerian 识别以及与前人工作的对应关系，**不得被说成是本文的新定理**。

## How to reproduce the short checks / 如何复现短检查

See `code/README.md` for the full list and recorded runtimes. A minimal, short check is:

```bash
cd code
python3 pbw_check.py
python3 sanity_random.py
python3 ambiguities.py
python3 lformula.py 10
python3 egf_check.py
```

These require Python 3; `lformula.py` and `egf_check.py` also need sympy. Longer jobs such as `kmodel_exact.py 8` (about 110 s) and `kproj.py 9` (about 554 s) were **not** re-run when this repository snapshot was prepared. `character.py` and `decompose.py` are exploratory and are not used in the paper.
