## A. Changes made because of prior work (Losev–Manin 2000, Dotsenko–Shadrin–Tamaroff 2022/23)

1. **New Section 11 "Relation to prior work"** (before "Computational verification", which moves from §11 to §12). Its contents:
   - What [LM, Thm 2.3] and [DST, Prop 2.4.2] prove, over a field of characteristic 0.
   - The dictionary: the multilinear part of U(k) equals tHyperCom(n), with G_T ↔ m_T, r_{i,j,S} ↔ R_{i,j}, and D-degree a ↔ homological degree 2a. There are no Koszul signs (all generators even, trivial S_t-action).
   - The consequence (7): dim U(k)^{(a,n−a)}_{[n]} = A(n,a), total n!.
   - The derivation of Cor 8.3, Prop 9.1 and Cor 10.4 from these.
   - A statement that our proof of these is an independent re-derivation.
   - Which results are not in [LM, DST].
   - A clearly labelled heuristic: representations of g ↔ commuting pencils, and LM's flat pencils d+λA.
2. **Abstract rewritten.**
   - The Eulerian/Hilbert-series step is stated as equivalent to known results (LM, DST).
   - The diamond-lemma proof is described as an independent self-contained derivation.
   - Novelty is limited to "We were unable to find the affirmative answer to Tao's question or this freeness statement in the literature."
3. **Introduction.**
   - "Status of the question" became "Status of the question and relation to prior work". It now gives:
     - the date (Nov 16, 2026);
     - the sources actually searched, and those not searched (Google Scholar, MathSciNet);
     - the equivalence of Cor 1.2(3), Cor 8.3 and Prop 9.1 with known results.
   - Novel results are listed only as "we were unable to find them in the literature; we make no stronger claim".
   - Cor 1.2(3) now carries a pointer to [LM, DST] and §11.
   - The outline mentions the alternative route via [LM, DST] and §11.
4. **Bibliography.** Two entries added; data checked against Crossref and the arXiv API:
   - [DST]: Comm. Math. Phys. 399 (2023), no. 3, 1439–1500, doi:10.1007/s00220-022-04577-6, arXiv:2112.06015.

   - Theorem numbers refer to the arXiv versions (LM v2, DST v3); this is stated in §11.
5. **Koszul remark (now Remark 10.5) replaced.**
   - The old remark only said the Segre-product identification "suggests…", with "details not included". DST Prop 2.4.3 does **not** give it: it concerns tHyperCom, i.e. U(k) in the generators G_T, not U(g) in the generators D_i, E_i. The new remark says so explicitly.
